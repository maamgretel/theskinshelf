How can we help?

Answers to the questions we get asked most.

Browse the shop, tap the + on any product (or open it and choose a quantity) to add it to your bag, then go to your bag and hit Proceed to checkout. Add your delivery details and place the order — that's it!

A flat ₱60 shipping fee applies per order, plus 12% VAT. Your full total is always shown in your bag and at checkout before you confirm.

Go to My orders from the menu. Each order shows a status timeline — Pending → Shipped → Delivered — so you always know where it is.

Yes — while an order is still Pending, open My orders and tap Cancel order. Once it's shipped it can no longer be cancelled.

Create an account and choose I'm a seller on the sign-up screen. You'll get your own dashboard to add products, manage stock and fulfil orders.

On the sign-in page, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and we'll send a code to reset it.
